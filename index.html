          About Maybe Real City

          Maybe Real City is a tiny pretend town that lives in your browser. Wander the districts, peek at the neighbors, and claim a little house with your name on it.

          This is a demo. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no servers. Every house you claim, every coin you earn, and every fresh coat of paint is stored only in this browser, on this device. Clear your browser data and the town forgets you — the pre-built neighbors stay, because they were born with the town.

          No tracking, no analytics, no ads. Just rooftops.

          Made by MeowSamOP DZN

          Privacy, plainly

          We collect nothing. Maybe Real City has no backend, no database, and no tracking scripts. Nothing you type or click ever leaves your device.

          What stays in your browser: the names you claim houses under, your Town Coin balance, quest progress, display settings, and home upgrades. This lives in your browser's local storage and never travels anywhere.

          What leaves your device: the 3D library and font load from public CDNs (unpkg and Google Fonts), which is how the page renders at all. That's the whole list.

          Want a clean slate? Clear this site's data in your browser settings and you'll arrive as a brand-new visitor.
